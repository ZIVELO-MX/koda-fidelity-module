# Koda Fidelity — Product Idea

## Overview

**Koda Fidelity** is a lightweight SaaS module for creating digital customer loyalty cards that can be added to **Apple Wallet** and **Google Wallet**.

The product is part of the **Koda POS ecosystem**, designed as a modular add-on for small businesses that want to improve customer retention without using physical stamp cards.

The MVP focuses on one simple loyalty model:

> Customers collect digital stamps after making purchases.

---

## Product Goal

Help small businesses turn returning customers into loyal customers through simple digital stamp cards.

The experience should feel:

- Fast
- Simple
- Mobile-first
- Wallet-native
- Friendly for non-technical business owners
- Easy to connect later with Koda POS

---

## Target Users

### Business Owners

Small businesses such as:

- Cafés
- Restaurants
- Barber shops
- Local stores
- Food stands
- Small service businesses

They want a simple way to reward returning customers without printing cards or managing complicated software.

### Customers

Customers who want to quickly save a loyalty card on their phone and receive stamps after each purchase.

No customer account should be required for the MVP.

---

## MVP Concept

A business creates a digital stamp card.

Customers scan a QR code at the business, open a mobile landing page, enter their name, and add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

When the customer pays, they ask the business to stamp their card. The employee scans the customer’s QR code and adds one stamp.

---

## MVP Customer Flow

1. Customer pays.
2. Customer asks to get their card stamped.
3. Business shows or scans the customer loyalty card QR.
4. The system adds one stamp.
5. The customer sees their updated stamp count in their wallet card.

---

## MVP Onboarding Flow

1. Customer scans a public QR code at the business.
2. Customer opens a mobile landing page.
3. Customer enters their name.
4. Customer taps:
   - Add to Apple Wallet
   - Add to Google Wallet
5. The card is added to the customer’s phone.

No login, password, or email verification should be required for customers in the MVP.

---

## Business Dashboard

The business dashboard should allow the business owner to:

- View total customers
- View active loyalty cards
- View total stamps issued
- Create loyalty card campaigns
- Customize logo and brand color
- Generate printable QR codes
- View recent stamp activity

Recommended navigation:

- Dashboard
- Loyalty Cards
- Customers
- QR Codes
- Branding
- Settings

---

## Loyalty Card Fields

Each digital loyalty card should support:

- Business logo
- Business name
- Primary brand color
- Customer name
- Current stamp count
- Required stamp count
- Expiration date
- QR code
- Reward description
- “Powered by Koda” watermark

---

## Card Design Direction

The card should feel inspired by:

- Apple Wallet
- Google Wallet
- Modern membership cards
- Boarding passes
- Minimal POS interfaces

Visual requirements:

- Large rounded corners
- Clean white surface
- Orange accents
- High readability
- Simple QR area
- Subtle watermark
- Minimal text
- Mobile-first layout

---

## Stamping Flow

Recommended MVP stamping flow:

1. Employee opens a web scanner.
2. Employee scans the customer card QR.
3. The customer card appears.
4. Employee taps “Add stamp”.
5. The stamp count increases.

For MVP, anti-fraud protection is not a priority.

---

## Offline Support

For the MVP, full offline support is not required.

Apple Wallet and Google Wallet can still display the customer card offline, but the business stamping interface should require internet.

Offline sync can be added later if needed.

---

## Not Included in MVP

The MVP should avoid:

- Native mobile apps
- Advanced campaigns
- Push notifications
- Coupons
- Cashback
- Points
- VIP levels
- Complex analytics
- POS integrations
- Multi-branch management
- Advanced anti-fraud
- Offline sync
- Customer accounts

---

## Product Positioning

Koda Fidelity is not a full CRM in the MVP.

It should be positioned as:

> Digital loyalty cards for small businesses, powered by Koda.

Simple promise:

> Replace physical stamp cards with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet cards.

---

## Landing Page Message

### Hero

**Turn returning customers into loyal customers.**

### Subheadline

Digital stamp cards for Apple Wallet and Google Wallet — powered by Koda.

### CTA

Start creating loyalty cards.

---

## Success Criteria for MVP

The MVP is successful if:

- A business can create a stamp card in minutes.
- A customer can add the card to their wallet in seconds.
- An employee can add stamps without friction.
- The card looks professional.
- The product feels useful even without full POS integration.
